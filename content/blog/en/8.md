This March, Yvo was invited by his high school alma mater, **Taipei Municipal Lishan High School**, to co-organize the Taipei Higher Education Fair.

The Higher Education Fair is an annual event hosted by the **Taipei City Government** at the World Trade Center, where high schools and vocational high schools in Taipei set up booths to publicly promote their educational philosophies and features to parents.

<img src="/blog_pdfs/parenting_world.jpeg" class="w-full h-auto rounded-2xl shadow-lg my-8" alt="Parenting Magazine" />

During the exhibition, Yvo explained Lishan High School's rich **school-based curriculum** and **project-based research** content to dozens of parents one by one, sharing his **international science fair experience** in the Science Education Center's Young Scientist Nurturing Program and his **international exchange portfolio** representing Lishan High School in Malaysia. Yvo applied his long-accumulated educational literacy and insights to comprehensively analyze the academic pathways for both humanities and sciences for parents, guiding anxious and confused students and parents.

At the same time, some parents were surprised to find that the news interview protagonist playing on the TV was Yvo right in front of them!

That was a feature interview recording of a younger Yvo by Da Ai TV, documenting how Yvo balanced his **music dreams** and **academic research** during his high school years under the 108 Curriculum and Lishan's nurturing.

> "I clearly remember those teenage days were tough. I often didn't sleep well, running between NTU Electrical Engineering, NTNU Biology, and Lishan labs to learn and study the application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in evolutionary biology in an era without LLMs. Everything I learned then, after many years, has become the nutrients for today." This is how Yvo mostly responded when many parents were curious about his high school experience.

<img src="/blog_pdfs/IMG_1442_Original.jpg" class="w-full h-auto rounded-2xl shadow-lg my-8" alt="Working Process" />

In addition to speaking for his own school, Yvo also established connections with principals and directors from academic institutions such as Neihu Vocational High School, Fuxing High School, and Kang Ning University during this exhibition. Yvo gained a deep understanding of the needs of teachers, students, and parents on-site, reminding himself that his **educational technology expertise** is meant to serve these people. Yvo will continue to dedicate himself to this: **making everyone fall in love with learning**.
