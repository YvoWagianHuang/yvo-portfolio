Yili Education now has a business card!

A good friend of Yili Education, Xiao Lin from Lins. digital studio, designed a business card for me. She used a cool blue-and-white color scheme to emphasize the restraint and rationality of technology, following the aesthetic of "less is more" to concisely present most of the information.

![](/images/20260617-105611.png)

In addition, she captured elements of gamification by incorporating a gamepad icon as well as a lively, overlapping layout, attempting to showcase Yili Education's sometimes wild and slightly crazy creativity through a touch of chaos.

I really love this business card. Here is the digital version for everyone to see:
![](/images/20260617-105721.png)
