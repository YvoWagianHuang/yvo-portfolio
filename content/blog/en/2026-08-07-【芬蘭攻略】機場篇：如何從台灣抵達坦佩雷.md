Hello, I'm Eve. When I was looking up transportation information from Taiwan to Finland, I ran into a lot of trouble. I'd like to summarize my experience here and share it with those who might need it.

***

Initially, based on my past travel experience, I looked up flight tickets on airline websites myself, but none were satisfactory. This was because I wanted Finland to be the first Schengen area country I entered, avoiding any administrative concerns.

Later, I heard that you can buy student tickets at [Goldtravel](https://www.goldtravel.com.tw/) in Taipei. As long as you present an International Student Identity Card (ISIC, which can be applied for in the same building), you can ask the travel agency to issue a connecting ticket. Not only is the price lower, but you can also bring two large pieces of checked baggage, and the agency can more efficiently arrange flights with reasonable layover times.

I recommend taking Finnair for the connecting flight (AY42). Try to avoid sitting near the middle engines; otherwise, like me, you might have trouble sleeping.

I ended up getting a ticket with a layover in South Korea. I hope that by the time you read this article, there are already direct flights from Taiwan to Finland! T_T  
> 💡 **Tip:** If this is your first time taking a flight with a layover, you will get two boarding passes in Taoyuan. If you have any questions, feel free to ask the Check-in counter. Good luck!

***

## Documents You Need to Bring

I was stopped and questioned at the transit counters and customs in both South Korea and Finland, so the following documents should be considered essential:

1. **Acceptance Letter**
2. **Printed AON Insurance Document**
3. **Rental Agreement**: Customs will ask where you are staying.
4. **Proof of Financial Means**: Customs didn't check it, but it will be needed later for your residence permit application.
5. **Migri Appointment Confirmation**: Taiwanese passport holders only get a 90-day visa exemption, so please make sure to book an appointment on the [Migri official website](https://migri.fi/en/home) beforehand.

Besides that, when entering Finland, there was some back-and-forth because the customs officer couldn't find my residence permit application in the system. I asked AI later, and AI suggested it might be due to issues with foreign name Romanization. I can't be sure about that, but anyway, I entered smoothly in the end.

***

## Airport to Tampere

You can take the [VR train](https://www.vr.fi/en) to reach Tampere. The train station is right next to Vantaa Airport. The airport is seriously insanely quiet!

You'll need to transfer once along the way. Finnish train routes are coded by letters, which is very easy to understand. When transferring, take the elevator upstairs to find your transfer platform—it's very intuitive.

VR trains offer private compartments, which I strongly recommend booking because the luggage areas in regular carriages can get completely packed. If you are traveling alone with two large suitcases, a small 2-person compartment is sufficient. If there are two of you with four large suitcases and you want more space, you can consider a 4-person compartment (though the 2-person compartment can still fit them).

> 📱 **Recommended App to Download: VR Matkalla**  
> It is recommended to download VR's official app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/fi/app/vr-matkalla/id1439811559) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=fi.vr.matkalla)) before departure. You can check timetables and buy tickets directly on your phone, which is extremely convenient!

After arriving at Tampere railway station, you can start your adventure! Later, you can go to the [Nysse](https://www.nysse.fi/en/) office to apply for a monthly pass, which gives you unlimited rides on trams and buses for a month (similar to a monthly MRT pass in Taiwan). For getting around the city center, buying an A-B zone ticket is enough. When you first arrive, you can use contactless credit card payments, but a single ticket costs 3 euros each time, so I highly recommend getting a monthly pass later on.

> 📱 **Recommended App to Download: Nysse Mobiili**  
> A must-have app for public transport in Tampere ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/fi/app/nysse-mobiili/id1112423333) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=fi.tampere.joukkoliikenne)). You can purchase single tickets or day tickets directly on your phone.
